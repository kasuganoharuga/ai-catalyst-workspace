# Pressure-Test Your Idea

Use this skill to evaluate whether a founder's idea has enough evidence, urgency, and strategic clarity to keep pursuing.

## Inputs

- Business idea
- Target customer
- Problem being solved
- Current evidence
- Key assumptions

## Output

Create a pressure-test verdict with the strongest arguments for and against the idea, key risks, and next-step actions.
