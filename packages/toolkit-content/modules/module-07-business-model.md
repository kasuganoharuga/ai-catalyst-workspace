# Module 07: Business model & revenue architecture

Turn the locked customer, solution, and competitive context into a concrete path to revenue —
inputs, model, pricing, and a 90-day cash-flow plan.

Canonical source: `skills/module-07-business-model/prompts/module-07-prompt-set.md`.

Not seeded yet.

## When to Use

After Module 4 (North Star / features) and ideally Module 6 (competitive / comparison context).
Do not re-ask who the customer is or what is being sold.

## Required Inputs

- Module 1 pressure-test context (proceed conditions)
- Module 2 beachhead
- Module 4 North Star and Feature Benefit Map
- Module 2 alternatives and Module 6 comparison / landscape where available
- Founder budget, weekly time, month-1 and month-6 goals

## Expected Outputs

- `Business-Model.md`
- `Pricing-Strategy.md`
- `90-Day-Cash-Flow.md`
