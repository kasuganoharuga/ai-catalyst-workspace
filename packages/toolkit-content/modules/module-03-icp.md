# Module 03: Ideal Customer Profile

Define the customer segment most likely to experience urgent pain and adopt an early solution.

## When to Use

Use this module when a founder needs to narrow broad customer hypotheses into a practical early ICP.

## Required Inputs

- Potential customer groups
- Pain intensity signals
- Buying or adoption context
- Existing interview notes

## Expected Outputs

- Primary ICP
- Secondary ICP candidates
- Disqualification criteria
- Interview targeting notes
